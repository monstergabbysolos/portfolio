Who I Am

I'm Joe Gabriel — a Mechatronics & Automation engineer now pursuing my MSc in Robotics & Intelligent Systems at NTU Singapore, passionate about building systems that bridge the gap between human intent and machine intelligence.

Nuclear · Instrumentation · IGCAR
Pipe Bend Ovality Measurement Instrument
Handheld field instrument developed for IGCAR Kalpakkam to measure pipe bend ovality in nuclear plant pipelines. Uses a TI LDC1612 28-bit inductance-to-digital sensor with a ferrite-core inductive position slider — replacing the legacy LGO oscillator circuit — for direct, high-precision measurement of Dmax, Dmin, and D₀. Computes ovality as W = (Dmax − Dmin) / D₀ × 100%. Features an ESP32-C3 SuperMini, 1.3″ OLED display, fractional calibration LUT, USB-C charging, and a 3D-printed Vernier caliper-style PETG enclosure (212 × 44 × 28 mm) designed for industrial field deployment.
