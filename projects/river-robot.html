River Cleaning Robot

Autonomous aquatic robot that detects, collects, and removes surface waste from rivers.

// The Problem

Why This Needed to Exist

River pollution — particularly floating plastic waste and surface debris — is a growing environmental crisis. Manual cleanup operations are labour-intensive, dangerous, and unable to scale to the volume of waste entering waterways. An autonomous solution is needed that can operate continuously without human supervision.

// The Solution

How It Was Built

An autonomous surface robot was designed to patrol river environments, detect floating waste using computer vision, navigate to it, and collect it into an onboard bin. The robot uses a catamaran hull for stability and a conveyor-based collection mechanism that scoops waste from the surface. Computer vision running on a Raspberry Pi classifies waste types and avoids natural debris like leaves or branches. A GPS-based patrol route ensures systematic coverage of the target water body.

// Technical Specifications

Under the Hood

HullCatamaran — stable, low-drag, reversible
DetectionCamera + CV model (trained on floating waste classes)
CollectionFront-mounted conveyor belt + rear storage bin
NavigationGPS-guided patrol + obstacle avoidance (ultrasonic)
ControlRaspberry Pi 4 running autonomy stack
EnduranceSolar-assisted battery — 6+ hours continuous operation
// Results & Impact

What It Achieved

In field tests on a controlled water body, the robot cleared surface waste at 3× the rate of a manual two-person team. The CV model achieved 89% classification accuracy on the target waste classes. The solar-assist system extended operational endurance to 6+ hours, enabling full-day autonomous deployment.
